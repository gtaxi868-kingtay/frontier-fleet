## How It Works

### Step 1: Sign Up as S4
1. Go to the registration page
2. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Rank
   - **Role: S4 (Logistics Officer)**
   - Email
   - Password
3. Click "Sign Up"

### Step 2: Automatic Self-Approval
After signing up:
1. You'll be automatically redirected to `/self-approve` page
2. You'll see a message: "First S4 Self-Approval"
3. Click **"Approve My S4 Role"** button
4. Your role will be approved instantly!

### Step 3: Access Full System
After self-approval:
1. You'll be redirected to the dashboard
2. You now have **full S4 access**:
   - ✅ All inventory modules
   - ✅ Role Management page (`/role-management`)
   - ✅ Can approve/reject other users' role requests
   - ✅ Analytics and Reports
   - ✅ Everything!

## After You're Approved

Once you're approved as S4, you can:
1. **Approve Others** - Go to `/role-management` to approve other users
2. **Manage Inventory** - Add/edit/delete items in all modules
3. **View Analytics** - See system-wide statistics
4. **Generate Reports** - Create and export reports

## Important Notes

- ⚠️ **Only the FIRST S4** can self-approve
- ⚠️ After the first S4 is approved, all other S4 requests must be approved by CO or existing S4
- ⚠️ The self-approval only works if NO approved S4 exists yet
